Deterministic motion
======

Suppose you are moving around with velocity $v$. How much will you have traveled after a small time $dt$? The answer is clearly $dx= v dt$, provided that the time increment is small enough and the velocity is sufficiently continuous. The key concept here is that if we zoom in enough into the moiton, the displacement is close to proportional to the time that has passed. Today, we will see how randomness breaks this very intuitive scaling - and of course how to remedy this.
